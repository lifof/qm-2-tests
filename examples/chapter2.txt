Chapter 2 - The Harbor Master

The next morning Mimi - only Joon called her that - pulled on her yellow raincoat and carried the tiny lantern down to the docks.

A tall woman in a navy greatcoat stood at the end of the pier, watching the fog. "That light," she said without turning around. "Where did you get it?"

"Who are you?" Mira asked.

"Captain Vale. Harbor master." She finally turned. "And that lantern belongs to the sea."
