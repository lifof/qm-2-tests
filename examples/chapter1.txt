Chapter 1 - The Lantern Shop

Rain had not stopped in Harbor City for eleven days. Mira Han pushed open the door of her grandmother's lantern shop, the bell above it ringing like it always had.

"You're late," said Joon from behind the counter, not looking up from the brass lantern he was polishing.

"The tram flooded," Mira said, shaking water from her sleeves. "Again."

Joon finally looked up. "Something came for you. No return address." He slid a small wooden box across the counter.

Mira opened it. Inside, wrapped in black silk, was a lantern no bigger than her palm. It was cold. Then, slowly, it began to glow.
